## 3. Data on your device

### What Dashnut stores

Dashnut stores the following in its private storage on your device:

- Game progress, best times and medals.
- Replays of your personal best runs for the optional personal best ghost.
- Collected acorns.
- Settings, such as language, sound, ghost visibility and haptics.
- The full game unlock status, including the product, whether it is unlocked, the source and the time of the last update.

The stored unlock status contains no payment data or Google account data. This locally stored information does not leave your device through the app, and I do not receive it.

Storing and accessing this information is strictly necessary to provide the game you requested. The legal basis for this device storage and access is section 25(2) no. 2 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act, or TDDDG.

The current version has no user accounts, analytics or tracking, and no third party SDKs for advertising or analytics. Its only third party component that communicates is the Google Play Billing Library on Android, used solely for the optional full game purchase described below.

### Deleting records and uninstalling

“Reset records” in Settings deletes your records and progress after you confirm. It keeps your settings and full game unlock.

Uninstalling the app deletes its data from your device. A purchased full game can be restored through “Restore purchases” in Settings.

I cannot access, retrieve or delete information stored only on your device.

### Device backups

On Android, app backups are disabled. Your saves are not included in Android or Google backups, so your progress stays on the device. The purchase itself can be restored through Google Play.

On iOS, depending on your device settings, iCloud device backups may include app data. Apple handles these backups under its own terms, and I have no access to them. Deleting app data or uninstalling the app does not determine what remains in separately managed iCloud backups.

### Android permissions

Dashnut declares four Android permissions:

- **Vibration:** for optional haptic feedback.
- **Google Play billing:** added by the Google Play Billing Library to offer, check and restore the optional one time full game purchase.
- **Internet access:** added by the Google Play Billing Library for those purchase functions.
- **Network status access:** added by the Google Play Billing Library for those purchase functions.

The game code itself makes no network requests. Dashnut does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or advertising ID.

### Links to the website

The About screen can open links, such as support or this policy, in your browser. The website sections of this policy then apply.

## 4. The optional full game purchase

### Google Play on Android

Dashnut is a free download with one optional one time purchase, the “full game”, which unlocks the remaining courses.

The purchase is sold and paid entirely through Google Play under Google’s terms. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google is an independent controller for your Google account, payment method and the purchase itself. See [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

To offer and check the purchase, Dashnut uses the Google Play Billing Library. When the app starts or returns to the foreground, and when you buy or tap “Restore purchases”, it asks Google Play on your device for product details and the price, and whether your Google account owns the full game. It also confirms a new purchase to Google Play using the purchase token Google issued.

This exchange takes place between the app and Google Play only. I do not receive it. Its legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, to provide the purchased content. Necessary access to the device is based on section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

An internet connection is needed only to buy or restore the full game. Without a connection, the game keeps working and an existing unlock stays valid. The unlock is removed only if Google Play reports that the purchase no longer exists, for example after a refund.

You can use “Restore purchases” in Settings with the same Google account, for example after reinstalling. “Reset records” does not remove the purchase.

### Order information available to me

I do not receive your payment details or Google account password.

As the seller, I can access order information in the Google Play Console. This includes, for example, the order number, date, product, price, taxes and fees, order status, and the buyer’s country or region needed for taxes. Google also lets me look up an order using its order number or the buyer’s email address, for example for support or refunds. Google decides which order information it provides under its own terms.

I use this information only to handle the sale, refunds, support requests and bookkeeping. Processing necessary to handle the sale and related requests is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Processing and retention required by tax and commercial law are based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

I retain this information for as long as applicable tax and commercial law require and otherwise only as long as needed for these purposes.

### Future App Store version

If the App Store version offers the full game, Apple will process that purchase under its own terms as an independent controller, and I will update this policy before that version is released.

### Spam protection with Cloudflare Turnstile

The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to protect against spam and abuse and maintain the availability of the contact service.

The Turnstile script loads directly from `challenges.cloudflare.com` when you first focus, click or touch, or type in the form, or when you submit it. It does not load merely because you view the contact page. It can therefore process technical information before you submit a message.

Turnstile processes your IP address, TLS fingerprint, browser information known as the User-Agent, and the site key and associated website origin. A TLS fingerprint describes technical characteristics of your connection. The website server also sends the verification token and your IP address to Cloudflare to validate the check. Your form message content is not sent to Turnstile for bot detection.

My legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, reflecting my legitimate interest in protecting the form from spam and abuse and keeping it available. To the extent that information is stored on or accessed from your device for strictly necessary protection of the contact service you request, section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG applies.

Cloudflare acts as my processor when protecting this website. Separately, Cloudflare acts as an independent controller when using the signals to improve Turnstile’s bot detection. Cloudflare states that this processing is based on its legitimate interests.

See the [Turnstile Privacy Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/) together with [Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for information about Cloudflare’s own processing, applicable retention rules and your rights. You can contact Cloudflare about that processing at [dpo@cloudflare.com](mailto:dpo@cloudflare.com). The provider details, DPA and international transfer safeguards above also apply to relevant Turnstile processing.

The form requires JavaScript and a successful security check. Using the form is not treated as consent. The automated spam check can block submission; direct email remains available without the form’s Turnstile check.
